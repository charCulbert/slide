The picture is the delay. The tall mark is your note. Every mark after it is a repeat at the moment it plays, left above the line, right below. Height is loudness in dB, so what you see is what you hear, and the marker shows where the tail ends. Drag above the line for left time, below for right, or drag the marker for decay. With sync the axis is in note values and times snap to the grid. Hold cmd or ctrl while dragging to bypass any snapping. Decay has three shapes: fade is feedback, flat gives a set number of repeats at one level then stops, swell makes them grow to the last one. Ping pong swaps sides on every lap. The two lines leak into each other by a fixed 15%, as a rotation, so the loop never loses energy on hold. Hold freezes whatever is in the lines at full level with the tone open, and keeps adding what you play. R is a tap turns the right line into a second head on the left one, heard but never fed back, which is the Echorec trick. Auto hold freezes the tail a quarter second after you stop playing and lets go the moment you start again, a sustain pedal you never press. Its threshold follows you: 30 dB under the loudest thing you played in the last few seconds, so it works the same for a whisper and a shout. Wobble is the medium: each shape is a recipe of read-head drift plus the loss and hiss that machine had, seeded so it plays the same every time. Everything runs in one AudioWorklet that mirrors the C++ engine, including its allpass diffusers.
